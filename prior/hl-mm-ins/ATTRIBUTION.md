## ins1 verdict (from reports/ins1/README.md)

Can a progressive capped-linear weather payout H(T) = $1 x min(max(T - K, 0), 6) be replicated from Kalshi's daily high-temperature bracket ladders at executable bid/ask (272 city-days, settled since 2026-07-24, five ladders)? On the three liquid ladders (NYC, Chicago/Midway, Miami) yes: hedge effectiveness at the own settlement station is 0.94 / 0.94 / 0.86 with overpay (premium at 24 h over realised payoff) of 17 % / 7 % / 0 %. On the thin ladders (Newark, Trenton) it fails at 24 h (asks about 3.4x the realised payoff, spread cost about 3.7 per unit) and is only executable near close. Geography dominates the error: hedging an exposure at a nearby station (LGA, JFK, FLL, OPF, cross EWR/TTN) with the settlement-station ladder gives HE 0.1-0.6 raw (0.30-0.68 after an in-sample offset/ratio fit) against about 0.9 at the own station. Binning is 10-30 % RMSE of the mean payoff, the Kalshi-settled bracket disagrees with the NWS ASOS max on 0-4 % of days, and depth (80-450 contracts per rung within 3c) caps a policy at roughly $100 per city-day. Chicago settles at Midway and closes 06:00Z. No local insurance-grade (30-day or longer, two-sided) ladders exist; only hurricane-count ladders qualify. Sell-side variant: +1.5c / +1.1c / 0c per contract all-maker markout with worst days 9-19x the mean, so not a bot.

## ins2 verdict (from reports/ins2/README.md)

Does a NOAA nClimGrid-Daily grid-cell settlement plus a station ladder close the geography basis? FAIL on both pre-registered legs, by a wide margin. Leg A: 0 of 1,206 cells (within 40 km of the five stations) have HE within 0.1 of their own station's HE (rule: at least 70 %); the best single cell is 0.24-0.41 below its station. Leg B: the pooled-to-individual variance ratio is 120-226 (about 0.74-0.82 times the number of cells; rule: below 0.5), because cell-minus-station residuals around one station are common-mode (rho 0.74-0.83), so a residual book does not diversify across cells. The nearest cell (0.7-2.5 km away) has HE only 0.37-0.71 against 0.89-0.96 for the station, so the gridded settlement adds day-definition and interpolation noise; aligning the grid label D to calendar day D+1 cut nearest-cell RMSE against ASOS from 4-5 degF to 1.1-1.7 degF. Test 3 (prelim vs final revision) is unavailable because NCEI keeps no vintages; `revision.py` waits for the September scaled file (about 2026-10-06). Temperature only (no rain), nClimGrid only (no PRISM), 65 days for NYC/MDW/MIA and 31 for EWR/TTN (15 of 272 city-days lack a grid value).
