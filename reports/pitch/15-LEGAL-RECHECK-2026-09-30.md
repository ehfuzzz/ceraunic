**1. New York and Kalshi.** Read 2026-09-30: https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/73700030/ (1:26-cv-06550); https://www.opn.ca6.uscourts.gov/opinions.pdf/26a0272p-06.pdf
Nothing is in force against Kalshi in New York: the remand motion has sat fully briefed since 28 Aug with no ruling or TRO on the docket, and the CFTC's suit against NY (1:26-cv-03404) was argued 14 Sep (secondary), no ruling on the docket through 28 Sep. The new fact is the 6th Circuit (*KalshiEX v. Schuler*, 25 Sep 2026) holding Kalshi's sports contracts aren't swaps, though its footnote 7 says weather swaps are, given their "historic and widely accepted association with economic risk."
Also: NY v. Polymarket US was removed to SDNY on 24 Sep (1:26-cv-08338). NY sued Coinbase and Gemini in April (secondary); we found no NY action against Robinhood or Crypto.com [unverified].
