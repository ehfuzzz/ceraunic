## Questions
- Q4a How much opposite-sign small-business temperature exposure cancels at station, ZIP and metro level?
- Q4b What does one uniform-price call auction per market-day leave for the pool, at what prices, and what does the protocol earn in fees?
- Q5a How much capital does the pool need per $ of the notional it carries, and does the capital model survive an out-of-sample backtest on Kalshi-era days?
- Q5b What do the house market-maker's fills earn after the Kalshi paper hedge (fees included)?
- Q5c How often would the stated revision policy settle on a CLI value that later changes the payout?

## Sample
- **Population:** Census ZBP 2023 detail (NAICS 2017) rows whose NAICS is in `naics_expanded` of the priors file (row 2, the demo ice-cream shop, is not a ZBP row and is excluded from the population); size classes < 50 employees (1-4, 5-9, 10-19, 20-49; midpoints 2.5, 7, 14.5, 34.5); suppressed "N" cells imputed as `est − Σ reported`, spread ≤ 2 per suppressed cell, remainder to the smallest suppressed class. ZIP → 2024 ZCTA gazetteer internal point → **nearest** of NYC (Central Park), EWR, TTN, MDW, MIA within 40 km; ZIPs without a ZCTA are dropped and counted. Revenue: `daily_rev = RCPT/EMPL` (SUSB 2022, state × NAICS6, all enterprise sizes; national row if the state row is missing or zero) × size-class midpoint / 365.
- **Hedge days:** every settled KXHIGHNY, KXHIGHCHI, KXHIGHMIA, KXHIGHTEWR, KXHIGHTTTN event whose market day is May 1 - Sep 30, with ≥ 6 rungs, exactly one "yes" result, a CLI high for the day, and a valid quote at the cutoff on every rung (valid = not (ask ≥ 0.99 and bid < 0.90) and ask > 0; last hourly candle ending ≤ cutoff). Per-station counts go to results.json. Phase 2 had not frozen its sample at this timestamp; this rule replaces "Phase 2's frozen sample" (deviation D1).
- **Weather history for capital:** GHCN-Daily TMAX 1951-2025 (station files `by_station/{ID}.csv.gz`), quality-flagged values dropped, converted to whole °F; NYC USW00094728, EWR USW00014734, TTN USW00014792, MIA USW00012839, MDW USW00014819 with ORD USW00094846 + monthly mean (MDW − ORD) offset from 1997-2020 before MDW's record starts. May 1 - Sep 30 only.
- **Split:** capital model (forecast proxy, error distribution, residual climatology, detrending) calibrated on 1951-2020 for the backtest; deployable numbers re-fit on 1951-2025 and labelled separately.

## Fixed choices
- **Instrument (station leg):** `H(T) = min(max(T − K, 0), 6)` $ per $1/°F unit, T = CLI daily high (whole °F), K = floor strike of the ladder's second bracket on day D. On-chain form: scalar unit on `[K, K+6]`, 1 $1/°F unit = 6 scalar units, scalar price ticks 1..99 cents (1 tick = $0.06 per $1/°F unit).
- **Residual leg (placeholder, Phase 3 not frozen):** R-A "metro minus station", `r = H(M) − H(T_S)` with the same K, `M` = equal-weight mean of GHCN-D TMAX over the Phase 3 temperature network plus S (NYC {LGA, JRB, TEB, EWR, JFK, CDW, MMU, HPN, FRG}; EWR {JRB, NYC, TEB, CDW, MMU, LGA, JFK, SMQ}; TTN {DYL, PNE, WRI, VAY, SMQ}; MDW {ORD, GYY, LOT, PWK, DPA}; MIA {OPF, HWO, HST, FLL, FXE}), quorum ≥ 60 % of listed members present, rounded to 0.1 °F. Scalar unit on `[−6, +6]`; 1 $1/°F residual unit = 12 scalar units. Days failing quorum drop from the residual leg only. Kalshi-era residual settles on GHCN-D values as fetched today (final values, not a T+N vintage).
- **Hedge units per business:** exposure `daily_rev × e_T/100 × max(T − T0, 0)`; where T0 is "—", exposure is linear `daily_rev × e_T/100 × T`. Units `h = −slope` of the min-variance regression of the exposure on H over 1951-2020 warm-season station days, per station × calendar month, with the historical K generated by the forecast-proxy rule below. Positive h = buys heat cover (bid on H); negative = sells heat cover (the long-heat business's "cool cover", offer on H). Hedge ratio 1. Order size = round(6·|h|) scalar units (station leg) and round(12·|h|) (residual leg, same sign); orders of 0 dropped. No Kalshi-era weather enters h.
- **Forecast proxy (long-history scenarios and hedge units only):** per station, detrended daily anomaly vs a 3-harmonic day-of-year climatology; lag-1 AR(1) forecast `F_D = clim_D + φ·anom_{D−1}`; historical K_D = round(F_D − 3.5) (the ladder is centred on the forecast; K is 3.5 °F below the centre of the four 2 °F brackets); scenario price `μ_D = E[H(F_D + ε − K_D)]` over the empirical AR residuals ε of the same station × month in the calibration window. This is a persistence forecast, weaker than the NWS/Kalshi forecast, so its error tail is a conservative stand-in.
- **Participation:** 2 % primary (0.5 %, 10 % secondary) of small establishments, binomial draws per (ZIP, NAICS, size) row on round(est), seed 20260930, fixed for the season. Secondary: participation weights by persistence (high 1.5×, medium 1×, low-medium 0.75×, low 0.5×, "—" 1×), renormalised to the same expected count.
- **Priors:** central run; ensemble = 1,000 draws, sectors independent, triangular(low, central, high) on e_T (and e_R for rain), seed 20260930, draws fixed across days; null as in M2.
- **Fair value at the cutoff:** station leg `μ = Σ_b p_b·H(v_b) / Σ_b p_b` with `p_b` = mid of the rung's last hourly candle ending ≤ cutoff, `v_b` = rung midpoint ("greater" rung: floor + 1; "less" rung: cap − 1), cutoff = close_time − 24 h (primary), close_time − 2 h (secondary). Residual leg `μ_r` = mean of r over the station × month in all years before year(D) with quorum (1951 → year(D) − 1), historical K from the proxy rule.
- **Hedger limits:** loading ℓ ~ U[0, 0.25] per business-day, seeded. Heat-cover buyer (bid): `μ(1+ℓ)`; heat-cover seller: `μ − ℓ(6 − μ)`. Residual leg: `μ_r ± ℓ·MAD_r` (MAD = mean absolute deviation of r, same climatology window; + for the buyer of r, − for the seller). Converted to scalar ticks: bids floor, offers ceil, clipped to 1..99.
- **Auction:** one open uniform-price call auction per (station, leg, day); the ONE clearing function `wi.auction.clear.clear` (shared with Phase 7 and Solidity): ticks 1..99; p* maximises min(D, S), then minimises |D − S|, then the (floor) midpoint of the tied tick range; price-time priority at the marginal tick; everyone trades at p*; integer escrow in 1e6 base units. Arrival order: businesses in a seeded random order, then the house (the house cutoff is after the businesses' orders), so the house is last in the queue at any tick.
- **House quotes:** fair ± capital charge. Charge per unit-day = `(CoC/365)·ΔES`, ΔES = Euler co-ES99 of one unit of the station's leg in the 30-day rolling-book scenario matrix (1951-2020), CoC 15 % primary (6 %, 30 % secondary). Inner ask tick = max(ceil(100·(μ + charge)/range), floor(100·μ/range) + 1); inner bid tick = min(floor(100·(μ − charge)/range), ceil(100·μ/range) − 1); three levels (inner, +1, +2 ticks away from fair), one third of capacity each. Capacity: $250,000 of full collateral per market day, split equally over the (station, leg) markets listed that day; full collateral per scalar unit = 1 − p for offers, p for bids. Orders beyond capacity stay unfilled and are counted.
- **Kalshi paper hedge:** house net station-leg position after the auction, hedged at the cutoff candle (buy YES at asks if the pool is short H, sell YES at bids if long), ins1 replication (contracts per rung = n·H(v_b), rounded to whole contracts), capped at 5 $1/°F units per city-day, taker fee ceil-to-cent(0.07·C·P·(1−P)) per rung order, hedge payout from Kalshi `result`. No hedge on KXHIGHTEWR/KXHIGHTTTN (2.4-2.7× overround) or on the residual leg.
- **Protocol fees (sweep):** 0 / 10 (primary) / 25 / 50 bps of notional (scalar units × $1) on every hedger fill; CoW-style: 50 % of each hedger's price improvement vs its limit, capped at 0.98 % of the fill's notional.
- **Capital rule:** `C_h = min(FC_h, max(ES99(bootstrap h-day loss), max historical h-day window loss))`, h ∈ {1, 5, 30} (30 primary). Book = the pool's average signed position per station × month × leg from the central auction run (after the hedge), revalued on joint-date 1951-2025 (deployable) or 1951-2020 (backtest) warm-season days; price in the scenarios = the scenario fair value (house markup excluded, conservative). Joint-date block bootstrap: 7-day blocks inside May-Sep, 10,000 paths, seed 20260930. `FC_h` = h × average daily full collateral. Junior (token) tranche = ES95 of the h-day bootstrap loss; senior = `C_h − junior`. Reported with and without detrending (detrended = each station's warm-season linear trend fitted on the calibration window, shifted to the 2026 level). Overlapping windows are not independent: 75 seasons, not ~11,000 windows.
- **Markouts (house fills):** `mo_settle = side·(payout − p*)·qty`, `mo_2h = side·(μ_2h − p*)·qty`, `mo_hedged = mo_settle + Kalshi hedge P&L (fees in)`; daily per station and pooled: mean, median, p10, p1, worst day / 5-day / 30-day, win rate, share of P&L from the 5 worst days, 95 % moving-block bootstrap CI (7-day blocks, 2,000 draws, seed 20260930); ins1 V1 as benchmark.
- **Revision (M4):** all CLI products for CLINYC, CLIEWR, CLITTN, CLIMDW, CLIMIA in the IEM AFOS archive for May-Sep of the Kalshi-era years; a product is preliminary if it says "VALID TODAY AS OF" / "VALID AS OF" for the same day; settlement vintage = latest non-preliminary product for day D issued ≤ D+1 12:00Z; comparison = latest product for day D in the archive. Units weight = hedger units traded that day in the central run; if a station-day has no trade, weight 1 in the unweighted share (both reported). Secondary: CLI vs later GHCN-D TMAX. nClimGrid prelim-vs-scaled only if Phase 1 plan 01-04 has written vintages by run time.
- **Rain:** no daily rain ladder is assumed tradable (Phase 2 not done); rain netting is descriptive: wet-day units `daily_rev × e_R/100` per business, same NR formula.

## Look-ahead controls (the only tests written)
- Priors hashed above before any population number.
- Hedge units, forecast proxy, residual climatology and the backtest capital model use 1951-2020 weather only (residual μ_r uses years < year(D)).
- μ uses only candles ending ≤ cutoff. Test: perturbing every post-cutoff candle and the realised settlement leaves every order, p* and fill unchanged.
- `clear()` money-failure property on 4,000 random books: Σ bid fills = Σ offer fills = Q*; no fill > qty; no filled bid below p*, no filled offer above p*; escrow in − refunds = Q*·1e6 exactly; never an unfilled marketable bid and an unfilled marketable offer at p* at once. Plus the 08-RESEARCH hand case (bids 10 @ 60 vs offers 5 @ 40 → p* = 50, fill 5).
- Settlement never reads a CLI product issued after D+1 12:00Z (fixture with a later correction).

## Known weak spots, stated before the run
- Priors are LOW confidence (two MEDIUM rows); Deryugina (2021) finds little sector heterogeneity, which is why M2 exists.
- The capital tail rests on ~70 independent warm seasons; the backtest has little power (expected 99 % exceedances ≈ 0.01·n).
- Kalshi depth: the 5-unit hedge cap is a guess from ins1's 5-20 units within 3c; no historical books exist before the hl-mm recorder.
- EWR/TTN have ladders only since 2026-08-27, so their sample is September 2026.
