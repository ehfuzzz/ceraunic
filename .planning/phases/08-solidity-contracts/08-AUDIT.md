## Seat rulings (2026-09-30 ~17:55Z)
- #1: market creation for a day is house/operator-only until the house cutoff, then permissionless; or the house's `openBook` may convert a non-auctioned market. Pick the smaller diff.
- #2: minimum order notional (e.g. ≥ 1 USDC escrow) and per-address open-order cap; house orders exempt from the 200 cap or reserved slots.
- #3: range-check qStar and order sizes (revert above uint128 / above the market cap).
- #4: `bind` reverts once the day is void; unbound buyer gets the full premium back.
- #5: `fundHouse` day must be within [today − 1, today + 2].
- #6: matured withdrawal requests must be claimed within 3 days of maturity, else they lapse back into the tranche (funds never leave the pool); cancel allowed before maturity. SPEC §8.2 change; engine + parity fixtures follow.
- Seat scope: withdrawDelay 12 d (Deploy + engine), guard `withdrawDelay ≥ houseReturnWindow + 2 d` and `unstakeCooldown ≥ houseReturnWindow + 2 d`; parity pool cases relative to withdrawDelay; Makefile anvil HOUSE_RETURN_WINDOW_S ≤ 1 d or delays raised so the guard passes; three tests to 12 days.

### Left undone and why
- API (`src/wi/api`) is untouched, since parity did not need it. It has no route to cancel or lapse a withdrawal. Its epoch (`clock._epoch`) will log `deferred (ClaimWindowClosed)` every day for a request past its window, until something calls `lapseWithdraw`. The anvil mirror has no `_WithdrawCancelled` / `_WithdrawLapsed` handler (the API never emits these events). `chainstate` does not compare the new constants (`MIN_ORDER_ESCROW`, `MAX_ORDERS_PER_TRADER`, `MAX_ORDER_QTY`, `CLAIM_WINDOW`). Follow-up for the API owner: add a lapse step to the API epoch plus the two mirror handlers.
- The per-address order cap can be beaten with many wallets: 10 wallets at ≥ 1 USDC each, all refunded, can still fill the 200 public slots. The protection that holds is the house exemption. What remains is griefing of other public traders, not a lockout of the house.
- The house is exempt from the minimum escrow, because residual legs can be 1 unit at a low tick. It stays a trust-T3 address: nothing but gas and the uint16 order count limits the house's orders.
- Finding #7 (house address drift, unsigned vintage hash, USDC blacklisting, house choosing which order binds, Lens growth): by design or mainnet items. Not changed.
