QUESTION (owner): does the CME settle reflect how the weather turns out, or the participants' need to hedge?

DATA
- Databento GLBX.MDP3 statistics + trades, 26 US monthly HDD/CDD roots, existing pull 2022-10-01..2026-10-24.
  Only records received before 2026-10-23 17:00Z are loaded. Only contracts whose month ended by 2026-09-30.
- Outcome: NOAA RCC-ACIS (GHCN-Daily) daily max/min, index = sum over the month of max(0, 65 - (max+min)/2) (HDD)
  or max(0, (max+min)/2 - 65) (CDD). Every day of the month must be present. If ACIS is incomplete, fall back to the
  CME final settle record (final flag, received Mon-Fri) for a trade date >= 2 business days after month end; else drop.
- Forecast: IEM archive of NWS GFS extended MOS (MEX), 00Z and 12Z runs, same 13 stations.

CONTRACT-DAY VALIDITY
- Keep a (contract, trade date) only if it has at least one settle record received Mon-Fri (Chicago time) with the
  "actual" flag. This drops Sunday-snapshot-only days. Price = final-flag settle if present, else latest received.

CLASSES (pre-expiry, trade date <= month end)
- A (trade class, primary): cleared volume > 0 AND trade date in [month start - 15 d, month end] AND settle differs
  from the previous valid settle of the same contract, with that previous settle at most 5 calendar days earlier.
- B (window mark): in the window, cleared volume = 0. Used at the fixed leads.
- A-gx (secondary): days with Globex prints; price = Globex VWAP; any lead.
- C (out-of-window marks): descriptive only.

FIXED LEADS
- L = 7 and L = 1 (MOS benchmark); L = 15 and 30 secondary (trend baseline only).
- Row = latest valid settle with trade date in [month end - L - 2, month end - L]. Actual leads are reported.

INFORMATION CUTOFF (assessment date T = the row's trade date)
- Observations: only days <= T-1.
- Climatology: sample windows (same calendar day +-3 d) must all be dated < T. 10-yr = the 10 latest sample years.
- Trend climatology: 30 sample years; warming slope = OLS on annual means of the 30 complete calendar years before
  T's calendar year; each sample year shifted by slope x (target year - sample year).
- MOS: day T's max and days T+1..T+6 max/min from the 00Z run of day T; day T's min from the 12Z run of day T-1
  (24 h projection). Missing run -> the run 12 h older (projection shifted); still missing -> climatology for that
  day. Counts reported. Assertion in code: no observation dated >= T enters any baseline.

BENCHMARKS (same rows, same T)
- (i) known-to-date + 10-yr climatology.
- (ii) PRE-REGISTERED: known-to-date + MOS for days T..T+6 + 10-yr climatology after.
  Daily DD from MOS = E[max(0, +-(65 - m))] with m ~ N((max+min)/2, s_k), s_k = 2.5 + 0.5k F, k = days ahead 0..6.
  Sensitivity: point plug-in (s = 0); trend climatology instead of 10-yr after day 6.
- (iii) trend baseline: known-to-date + trend climatology.

STATISTICS
- e = price - outcome (sign flipped vs 7b; e > 0 = futures buyers paid more than the outcome).
- d = price - benchmark.
- Row-weighted mean; SE = CR1 cluster-robust SE of that same row-weighted mean, clusters = season
  (HDD season = Oct..Apr, named by the October year; CDD season = calendar year). Month-clustered SE also shown.
- 95% CI = mean +- t(G-1, 0.975) SE. MDE (80% power, 5% two-sided) = (t(G-1,0.975) + t(G-1,0.80)) x SE,
  normal 2.8 x SE also shown. G < 20 clusters: no z or t statistic is printed; results are "direction only".
- RMSE of price and of each benchmark vs outcome on the same rows.
- Primary sample: pre-registered cities Chicago (H2/K2), New York (H4/K4), Cincinnati (H3/K3). Secondary: 13 cities.

VERDICT RULE (7b, unchanged except the sign convention)
- "hedging pressure": class-A mean(e) has the same sign in >= 2 of 3 cities AND the pooled 95% CI of mean(e)
  excludes 0 AND |mean(price - bench ii)| >= 2 SE.
- "prices the outcome": |mean(e)| <= 1 SE AND RMSE(price) <= RMSE(bench ii).
- otherwise "inconclusive". Also applied to the class-B marks at L = 7 and L = 1.

"LEAN COLD" SENTENCE (old: CDD trades -8.9 vs trend, 3.7 SE)
- Survives only if, on fixed class A (13 cities, CDD), the 95% season-cluster CI of mean(price - trend baseline)
  excludes 0 AND the sign matches in >= 2 of the 3 pre-registered cities. Otherwise withdrawn.

DEVIATIONS from 7(b) known now
1. Trade class tightened (in window + settle moved), snapshot-only days dropped (reviews F1, F2).
2. Outcome = ACIS index instead of the CME final (19 extracted finals wrong, F3/A5).
3. Clusters = season, not month (F4, A3).
4. Benchmark (ii) uses GFS extended MOS (MEX), not NBM: the NBM text archive is shorter and the brief allowed either.
5. The 2010-22 extension is not run (data thin before mid-2024, F2; would need a re-pull).
6. The 13-city pool and the trend baseline are additions, reported as secondary.

2026-10-24T22:44:13Z — reporting-lag correction (before inspecting scored redo results)
The raw audit found positive cleared-volume records commonly arrive on the next calendar day (median receipt
37.7 hours after reference-date midnight). The first implementation's same-day volume cutoff is therefore
withdrawn. Use the latest pre-seal cleared volume for the EX-POST class label only, with no volume entering
the baseline or price. Missing volume means no reported positive volume, not verified zero activity. Keep the
same-day cutoff for settlement prices. The first implementation is superseded and not used for conclusions.
Write the raw-feed audit artifact before computing the final scored comparisons. An implementation-only
speedup uses date-ordinal lookups in climatology, retaining the explicit observation cutoff.

13..17 complete annual observations within the trailing 30-calendar-year window, not 20. Use the available
complete years (minimum 10; otherwise drop the trend comparison), never fill a partial year with future
